Frequently Asked

Common Questions About Our Legal Services

Short answers to what clients most often ask before starting a consultation.

Where is Jad Alabdullah Law Firm located?

The firm is based in Al Shati, Jazan 82726, Saudi Arabia, and serves clients across the Kingdom, including remote consultations by phone and WhatsApp.

What types of cases does the firm handle?

Commercial litigation, financial and negotiable instruments disputes, real estate matters, enforcement disputes, and corporate governance and advisory work for companies and individuals.

How do I book a legal consultation?

Send your request from the contact page, or reach the firm directly by phone or WhatsApp between 8 AM and 3 PM, Sunday to Thursday.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. All matters are handled under professional confidentiality obligations, and published case files are redacted so that party names and private data are concealed.

Does the firm work with companies as well as individuals?

Yes. The firm represents companies, establishments, and individuals, and provides ongoing advisory retainers covering contracts, governance, and regulatory risk.

What should I prepare before the first consultation?

Bring the contract or claim documents, any official correspondence, and a short timeline of events. Organised documents make the initial legal assessment far more accurate.
